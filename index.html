    How to use

    Today shows five new words every day. No word is repeated until the whole list has been covered.

    Search works both ways: type a word to find its card, or type a word from a meaning (such as angry or praise) to find words with that meaning.

    History keeps every day's words. Words you got wrong in a test are listed at the top under Words to revise.

    Use the strip on the left to move between Today, History, Practice Test, Quiz and Search. This page opens from the About button at the top.

    Practice Test tests you on words from the whole list. Quiz tests you only on the words you have already learnt. Both have three kinds of question: word → meaning, meaning → word, and fill in the blank.

    Every word has Oxford and Cambridge buttons that open its entry in those dictionaries. Tap a related word to look it up too.

    Your words, scores and revision list are kept only on this phone.
